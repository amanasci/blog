Today during the group meeting a question came up about learning resources for LLM research and engineering. I thought it would be good to share some of the resources that I have found useful in my journey of learning about LLMs. This is not an exhaustive list but these are some of the resources that I have found useful. Though when we include HackerNews the list becomes quite exhaustive. HackerNews has been my go-to source for learning about new developments in the field of LLMs. I have found some of the most interesting and insightful discussions on HackerNews. Infact on most days I sleep scrolling through HN. So, if you are interested in learning about LLMs, I would highly recommend following HackerNews and participating in the discussions there. I'll try to keep this list updated as I find new resources and as the field evolves. So, without further ado, here is the list of learning resources for LLM research and engineering.
